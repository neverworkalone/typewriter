## Scope and result

- Scope: all 69 `data/meanings/c01_NNN.jsonl` shards.
- Baseline: 49,280 distinct lemmas and 69,005 Meaning rows; 9,037 lemmas had multiple Meanings and were reviewed against their bound canonical source senses.

- Unchanged rows in the reviewed multi-Meaning lemmas are KEEP decisions. The spec preserves all remaining source-sense membership.
- `data/canonical/` was used as read-only source evidence and is unchanged.

## Editorial review provenance

- The author performed source-bound semantic checks in this primary agent context. This is an **agent self-check**, not an independent or human review.
- Each MERGE was checked against the lemma and every included canonical sense. Distinct homonyms and unrelated referents were retained; natural literal/figurative, general/specific, grammatical, and same-concept technical uses were grouped where the source meanings support one lexical identity.
- Exact duplicate drops were checked for same-lemma, same-POS, identical source definitions and a live same-lemma target. All other source senses remain assigned to a Meaning.
- C01_044 count was corrected after comparing the review ledger with the shard: its actual count is 130 multi-Meaning lemmas. Groups 101-130 were source-checked after the discrepancy was found.
- C01_069 was completed after reviewing all 75 multi-Meaning lemmas.
