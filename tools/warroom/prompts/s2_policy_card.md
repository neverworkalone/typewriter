## Shared Stage 2 decision policy (#2544)

- Do not discover candidates or revisit Stage 1 production. For **each listed candidate**, judge its exact source Sense/full gloss against existing approved peers (`P`), saved decisions (`Q`), and other same-Sense candidates. **Stage 2 owns final editorial admission**; a semantic connection by itself is not enough.
- **Two gates before assigning Type/Tier:** establish the actual source-Sense connection, then decide whether this candidate earns a separate place among that Sense's writer-facing choices. **During initial production**, resolve the applicable Drop reason and concrete Keep justification as part of this decision, without waiting for a reviewer to identify suspect approvals. This is not an approve-first/review-later pass or another session: use the supplied context and existing compact output, without per-candidate explanations or extra tool calls.
- **Test the Drop reason against the Keep justification:** check whether the candidate merely repeats a choice, restates the gloss without adding expression value, names a generic topic or incidental object/place, or depends on an unsupported multi-hop association. For a suspected case, identify what specific wording, meaning, grammatical use, register, mood, image, action, scene or sensation it contributes from the exact Sense, and whether that contribution actually answers the objection. Mere connection or possible co-occurrence does not justify Keep; a generic label does not prove Drop. Use `D x` for no connection/wrong Sense and `D e` for a connected but expression-free choice; keep a defensible distinct path. No Keep/Drop quota, and no low-Tier parking for a deserved Drop.
- **Compare same-Sense peer choices before admission:** compare with the closest relevant choices in `P`, `Q` and other same-Sense candidates. Determine whether a noun/verb ending change, nominalization, trivial derivation, repetition or spacing variant merely duplicates a choice (`D e`), or offers genuinely different meaning, grammatical use, register or imagery (Keep). Similarity or shared spelling/root alone is not redundancy; do not collapse useful alternatives to one representative. Do not re-adjudicate approved/saved peers or treat multiple forms (`fm`) inside one ID Relation as separate duplicates. Judge only supplied candidates, not a second Stage 1 form scan, string blacklist or history sweep.
- **Proper nouns are excluded both ways (#5745):** if the source Sense's full gloss names a specific person (real name, pen name, art name), place, institution, organization, or work/title, answer `D e` for **every** candidate of that Sense. Likewise answer `D e` for any candidate whose meaning here is such a proper noun (e.g. 김성수, 동아일보, 고려대학교, a specific work title), even when the relation is factually true. Do not admit it as an ID, text-only, or reverse Relation, and do not relink it to another Sense or spelling; use `x` only for a genuinely unrelated or wrong-Sense candidate, not for this policy. Judge by the exact Sense/gloss, not spelling or POS: `w265063-s1` 인촌¹ (김성수의 호) is a proper-noun source, `w265064-s1` 인촌² (이웃 마을) is ordinary. Class nouns such as 사람·도시·학교·소설·영화·작가 remain normal vocabulary, and a same-spelling candidate may still bind to its ordinary target Sense.
- **Protect useful creative paths:** keep intelligible but rare/indirect or low-priority imagery when it genuinely helps writing. `깜빡대다` memory Sense→`가물가물`, light Sense→`점멸/명멸` are positive controls. `어정대다→낙타` is **not an automatic Drop**: decide its value from the source Sense and plausible creative path. Judge `팔딱하다→심장` by its actual Sense. A different POS, gloss mention, or unfamiliarity never settles admission alone.
- **Drop code in the existing `D` answer:** use `D e` when a candidate connects to the exact source Sense but has no independent writer-facing expression value; use `D x` when it is unrelated or bound to the wrong Sense. Stage 2 must never output `z`, `f`, prose, `editorial:`, or `edit`; `z` is reserved for Stage 3 orphan accounting and `f` is reserved for the Supervisor's #2874 technical-failure path. The gloss-name counterexample check applies to `x` and is bypassed for `e` and `z`; the Supervisor's `f` path is non-editorial and bypasses that check. Decide the meaning and expression value from the source-bound evidence, then output only `x/e` or a relation; do not bypass verification or alter persisted JSONL fields.
- For a **kept** candidate, choose its actual type, not default `association` or fold `scene`/`sensory` into it or `mood` into `near`.
  - `near`: lexical neighbor or nuance close to the source; `mood`: emotion, atmosphere, or tone.
  - `scene`: situation/place/scene; `sensory`: sound/smell/perception/experience; `action`: related act or motion (an exact ID target must be a verb/expression Sense; for a noun-only target use the text-only `none T<a>,...` form).
  - `association`: broader concept/object/topic when no more specific type fits. Do not use `direct` or `antonym`.
- Only for kept candidates, set Tier (`relevance: 1..9`) relative to `P`, `Q` and same-source/UI-group peers: `near`/`mood` = 말의 결; `scene`/`sensory`/`action`/`association` = 연상. Tier 1 is near-substitutable; 3 strong; 5 useful; 7 broader; 9 peripheral **but still valuable**. Even values interpolate. 연상 Tier 1 needs a real informal synonym candidate. Never rank for quota or to avoid a deserved Drop.
- For an ID↔ID relation, the stored edge is shown both ways; consider **both directions' writer value** before admission. Do not re-adjudicate already-approved duplicate/reverse-covered relations. Bind an ID only after comparing *every supplied same-spelling target Sense/full gloss* and finding an exact match; otherwise keep an admitted target text-only and list every target alias shown. If none exists, omit the target field.
