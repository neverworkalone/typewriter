**Confirmed Skip whole-Drop categories (#10171 §3.2/§3.3, #10265).** This rule applies only to a Sense whose common pre-selection is confirmed Skip: in S2/S3 a trailing `s` field marks it and an unmarked Sense is never judged by this rule; in D1 every displayed Sense is already a confirmed Skip (no marker is shown). `s` alone is **never** a Drop reason. For a confirmed Skip Sense, read its exact lemma, POS and full gloss and ask only: does **this Sense itself** clearly mean one of these 11 categories?

1. **Made product / facility kind:** a kind of manufactured, cooked, compounded or processed article or product, or a building, facility or partitioned space set up for a purpose (dishes, medicines/prescriptions/dosage forms, fabrics/materials, ceramics, printed editions, devices/parts, rooms/halls/warehouses). Not the act or process of making, and not a general result or state.
2. **Rule-defined event or form:** the name of a game/sport, a specific rite/ceremony/service, or a musical, dance or verse form, i.e. the form itself, not every motion performed inside it.
3. **Formal system / classification and its items:** a named system or classification, or an item whose meaning is fixed by its definition, notation or rule: measuring units, mathematical notation/formal concepts, musical items (keys, chords, note values), game-rule items, languages/scripts, calendar/era terms, and words used as a fixed drill command or work/contact signal (not interjections or emotional exclamations in general).
4. **Specialist identification of substance, organism, terrain or medicine:** an element or compound, a rock/soil/landform kind distinguished by composition or origin, a biological species or sub-classification, a specific disease or medically defined pathological state, or a specialist anatomical part or tissue name.
5. **Fixed institutional name:** an office or post under a system, an official document/register/regulation, a legal offence or crime class, a named court procedure, and an occupation, position, organization or industry named by its duties, qualification, makeup or production/service activity (including modern ordinary jobs and organization types). Not a person's character, situation or relationship.
6. **Academic field / theory / school / research method:** a discipline or sub-field, or the specialist sense naming a particular theory, school or research method.
7. **Proper name or specific human group:** a company, brand or other proper name (also the existing #5745 proper-noun exclusion), or the name of a specific ethnic, tribal or national group, or a member identified by that ethnicity or nationality.
8. **Proverb or idiom, without exception:** every proverb and every fixed idiomatic expression, regardless of length, familiarity, moral or a near-synonym gloss. POS `expression` alone does not prove an idiom.
9. **Purely grammatical function:** an auxiliary sense that only adds conjecture, repetition, benefaction, completion or similar grammar to the preceding word.
10. **Pure number / order / quantity unit:** a sense that is only a number, an ordinal, or a counter or measure of how many or how much.
11. **Reference-only sense:** a gloss with no independent meaning that only points to another headword (`→ 다른 표제어.`, optionally followed only by a spelling-origin note). Not abbreviations, archaic or dialect forms in general.

**Boundaries:** judge what the exact Sense means, never the field label, POS, suffix, spelling or a word inside the gloss, and never an adjacent Sense or a homograph. A specialist field alone is **not** a Drop: a word that expresses a real action, state, relation or change stays under the normal criteria even when used in law, chemistry or physics. For example, 부합 `w432385-s2`, 분별 `w436139-s4` and 응고 `w263522-s2` are not whole-Drop. Conversely, a Sense that does fit a category (a disease, a named procedure, a research method, an idiom) is not exempted because it describes an action or state. Read homographs by their own Sense: 유제 `w261296-s1` (an oily medicine) fits category 1, while 유제 `w261298-s1` (working hide or thread soft) does not. 배붙이다 `w136974-s1` (a real wrestling motion) is not category 2 or 3. 영화관 `w239361-s1` (a view of film) is not a facility. 매장꾼 `w112900-s1` (burial worker) is category 5, but 매몰자 `w112205-s1` (a buried person's state) is not. If the fit is unclear, apply the normal criteria; do not stretch a category to methods/techniques, kinship terms, honorifics, field-relational `-적` senses, belief/myth beings, variant forms or natural phenomena in general.

Once a confirmed Skip Sense clearly fits, it is whole-Drop: keep **no** Typewriter-authored relation of it because of usefulness, Tier, reverse value or a `p` counterpart. STDICT `direct/antonym` is never shown or touched here.
