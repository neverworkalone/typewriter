## Adjudication (AI self-check; limits stated)

- **Design.** The sample is predeclared and deterministic: seed `issue-274-v1`, up to 10 usages per stratum (each usage in exactly one primary stratum, first match in this order), ranked by `sha256(seed|stratum|id)`. It covers all-three agreement (Kiwi-ambiguous and Kiwi-clean), each one-vs-two disagreement, three-way disagreement, derivation/lemma/POS-mismatch rows, unsupported/error rows, and 10 of the 49 baseline-unheld controls: **85 usages**, 83 with an AI self-check truth, **0 independently verified**, and 2 `truth_unknown` (noun-vs-adverb boundary).
- **Method — read this first.** Truth is the dictionary-form lemma/POS of the usage in the authorized local original context (data/reference corpus; no text in Git), recorded **before** viewing any analyzer output (the outputs of six rows were viewed after their truth was decided; listed in the adjudication file). It is an **AI self-check by the assigned primary agent** (repository rule: no subagents, no other-model review). It is *not* human or independent adjudication, so **no reading is called "verified"**: records carry status `ai_self_check`, and the `verified` status is reserved for an independent, analyzer-blind adjudication with recorded provenance (the validator rejects a `verified` record without it). The independent adjudication required by issue #274 §6 is **outstanding**. A reading *agrees* with the self-check when its best path contains the recorded lemma/POS (or the root of a derived predicate). One analyzer was never used as truth.
- **Bounds.** The sample is stratified, not random over the cohort, so the agreement figures above are within-sample self-check rates, not accuracy or a population estimate (the all-agree strata are random draws of 10 from 207 / 76). Wilson intervals are shown; they are wide.

## Decision

1. **Quality.** Under `provider-resolution-v1` neither fallback order verifies any additional correct resolution (gain 0, held rows unchanged), because best-only readings cannot clear `analysis_ambiguous`. Accuracy is **not established**: against the AI self-check Kiwi's best path agrees at 84.9% (75.0%–91.4%), Khaiii 73.7% (62.8%–82.3%), MeCab 66.7% (55.4%–76.3%); the intervals overlap, the sample is stratified and nothing is independently verified.
2. **Pareto.** Kiwi: highest self-check agreement, ≈1.4 s per process; Khaiii and MeCab: ≈10× faster to start but lower self-check agreement and no N-best/derivation. No provider order is shown to improve on Kiwi-only; the fallbacks are cheap, so the question is signal value, which is unproven.
3. **Conclusion: inconclusive; retain Kiwi-only as the factory default.** The agreement signal looks promising (see the hypothetical rule) but nothing is independently verified. Resolving it requires an independent, analyzer-blind adjudication of a larger rights-cleared sample and an explicit owner decision on the admission trust model (a successor issue); this issue changes none of it.

## Limits

No independent adjudication (issue #274 §6 outstanding; all truths are AI self-check); 2 `truth_unknown`; stratified (non-random) sample; one host and one run set; Docker Khaiii not measured; the extractor's share of 319 ambiguity holds is indeterminate; the baseline is the still-open, unmerged PR #270 head commit by the owner's chat instruction, which issue #274 does not yet record; the v1 cohort is 500 usages, not 500 lemmas, and is unrelated to the #275 v2 contract.
