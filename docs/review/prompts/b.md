# B Candidate PR — single-scene exploration

Read `candidate.md` first; `REVIEW.md` is the shared authority.

- Route only a Candidate-only `[B/<model>] <SSS>_<BB>` PR whose changed file is `..._b_<model>.jsonl`.
- B's producer imagines **one** concrete source-Sense-bound scene and extracts usable words, actions, senses, objects and compact non-narrative images (#860). The scene is an internal production method, not a PR artifact. **Do not** demand a second imaginary scene, evidence receipts or disclosure of a private scene; do not claim to prove the private scene from output alone.
- Risk-first inspect the submitted Candidate forms against the exact bound Sense. Especially distinguish genuine short scene expressions (preserve) from copied scene narration, predicative clauses, gloss attribute+category restatements, trivial field→person derivations and source-lemma containment (reject clear failures only).
- Apply `candidate.md`'s one-pass mechanical checks, jamo-only rule and narrow follow-up. No independent review of private generation steps or full per-candidate semantic census.
- **Proper nouns (#5745):** a source Sense whose gloss names a specific person, place, institution, organization or work must have `candidates: []`, and no candidate may mean such a proper noun (e.g. 김성수, 동아일보, a work title), even if factually related. Judge by exact Sense/gloss (인촌¹ empty vs 인촌² normal), not spelling; class nouns such as 사람·도시·소설·작가 stay valid. A surviving proper-noun candidate is a form-gate defect within the normal review scope.
