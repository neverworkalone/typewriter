# Candidate-only PR review — shared A/B/C/D gate

Read the root `REVIEW.md` at this exact PR HEAD before this file; it controls verdict, follow-ups, CI separation and review submission. This file defines only **Candidate review** of the data PR, not production directions. In an ordinary Candidate-only review read this file plus exactly one of `a.md`, `b.md`, `c.md`, `d.md`; do not load the other method cards.

## Inputs and one-pass verification

- Confirm the **complete PR changed-path list**. Use this route only if material changes are one method's `data/relation-candidates/<SSS>/<SSS>_<BB>_<method>_<model>.jsonl`. Mixed PRs go through `code.md`, not a Candidate-only shortcut. A title alone is not sufficient.
- Determine the matching Canonical file directly as `data/canonical/stdict-<SSS>.jsonl`. Candidate Batch `01` is the first 500 records of that shard; Batch `02` is the remaining records. Do not search filenames by guessing directories (`data/canonical` contains `stdict-XXXX.jsonl` files).
- When necessary to substantiate schema/count/order validity, run the existing contract **once**: `node scripts/relation/candidate-contract.mjs data/canonical/stdict-<SSS>.jsonl data/relation-candidates/<SSS>/<SSS>_<BB>_<method>_<model>.jsonl`. Use concrete file names, not literal angle placeholders. Reuse trustworthy existing evidence instead of repeating identical checks.
- Audit all changed records and Senses for membership/order and the required independent **whole-batch** source-lemma↔candidate containment check (normalize spacing/hyphens, both directions); use one extraction/Canonical lookup. Review suspected form failures risk-first with bound `lemma`, `sense_id`, `gloss`. Report the scope actually checked, and exact `(record_id, sense_id, candidate)` blockers. Do not read unchanged producer/validator/SQLite internals, CI, or all unrelated review guides for a data-only batch.

## Stage 1 Candidate form gate

**Technical skips:** Do not review a small number of Supervisor-recorded `Technical skip` cases in Candidate PRs.

**Proper-noun editorial exclusion (#5745):** A source Sense denoting one specific person, place, institution, organization or work must retain its record/Sense but have `candidates: []`; a candidate denoting such a proper noun is ineligible even under an ordinary source Sense. Review the exact source/target meaning, not spelling alone (`인촌¹` vs `인촌²`); class nouns such as 사람·도시·학교·소설 remain eligible. C `PROPER_NOUN_SKIPPED` and D `P` legitimately have no VERIFIED evidence and are **not** Supervisor technical skips. Apply the normal initial risk-based discovery and narrow follow-up review scope; do not require a second full semantic census.

**Stage 1 Candidate form gate (all A/B/C/D, including 500-record factory batches):** Candidate PRs are not judged solely by Candidate v1 shape, source-sense matching, or whether `candidates: []` is permitted. **Preserve intelligible individual WORDS and concise meaning-extending SCENE/IMAGE/SENSORY/EMOTION/ACTION PHRASES; strictly reject mechanical gloss copies, disguised clauses and SENTENCES.** This is a *Stage 1 output eligibility check*, **not** Relation Factory Stage 2's relation-worth/Tier/Drop selection.

- **Independent words:** preserve a source-sense-bound, intelligible path even if it is weak, ordinary, creative, cross-POS, or unfamiliar. Do not require a direct synonym or a minimum frequency. A word is not junk just because it is context-dependent.
- **Source lemma ↔ candidate containment (mandatory zero):** If either side contains the other after the defined whitespace/hyphen normalization, **reject the candidate without exceptions**, even when it is a separately attested word, an independent concept or a potentially useful image. Examples: `건축 → 건축가`, `반송 → 반송파`, `눈 → 눈빛`, `불 → 불씨`, `꽃 → 꽃말`, `개 → 개집`. This rule is distinct from shared-stem/suffix/part-of-speech similarities **without actual containment**, which still need source-Sense judgment. Exact source lemma and annotation-only repeats remain forbidden.
- **Clearly trivial morphology/derivation:** the producer and initial Candidate reviewer reject `깜빡대다 → 깜빡이다` (simple form/ending variant), `조퇴하다 → 조퇴` (same-concept nominalization), `팔딱하다 → 팔딱팔딱` (unhelpful repetition), as well as `건축 → 건축가` above. These are narrow Stage 1 eligibility examples, **not** a spelling/regex rule against all related stems, word classes or repeated syllables. Debatable indirect connections such as `어정대다 → 낙타` stay eligible for Stage 2 to judge.
- **Multiword lexical expressions, short phrases, idioms, and concatenated expressions without forbidden source-lemma containment:** the strict check targets **mechanical gloss restatements, unspaced ordinary syntactic fragments, arbitrary modifier+lemma expansions and disguised narration**, **not** artistic merit or phrase count. Preserve compact sense-bound expansions into a scene, image, sensation, action or emotion even when the wording is ordinary (`수괘` [frost] → `겨울 아침`, `산뜩` → `가슴 철렁`). Reject a plain gloss attribute+category (`가는소금` → `하얀 소금`, `가는솜방망이` → `노란 꽃`) or modified lemma (`걸음걸이` → `느린 걸음걸이`) that merely repeats the source without a different association. Whitespace or concatenation alone neither qualifies nor disqualifies a phrase. **Do not demand unusual originality, demonstrably superior imagery, or Stage 2 admission value in Stage 1.** A genuinely debatable compact expression stays for Stage 2 rather than causing repeated `-1`.
- **Complete sentences, predicative clauses, narratives of a particular action/event/procedure, and sentence fragments masquerading as nominal phrases are strictly forbidden in final `candidates[]` (zero exceptions)**. E.g. `검지기 → 누전이 되면 경보가 울린다`, `격벽 → 격벽을 여는 데 에너지가 필요하다`, `겅중 → 긴 다리로 뜀`. Removing punctuation, changing endings, or appending `scene/process/thing` does not make a narrated event eligible. An independent verb lemma such as `떨다` remains an allowed **word**, not an event description.
- **Mandatory independent containment/derivation gate (initial Candidate review only):** From the actual changed Candidate file and matching Canonical lemmas/Senses, extract **all** source-lemma ↔ candidate pairs where either normalized string contains the other (ignoring spaces/hyphens), then verify there are **zero** matches. **Every match is a Stage 1 `-1` blocker without exceptions**; do not override for synonyms, dictionary status, semantic independence or writer-facing imagery. Both `개 → 개집` and `반송 → 반송파` must be excluded. Independently search shared-stem, suffix/ending and near-form derivatives **not caught by containment**, e.g. `발라드 → 발라더`, and assess their actual source Sense for triviality; `마련하다 → 마련`, `박제하다/박제되다 → 박제`, `발버둥질 → 발버둥`, `반자동 → 반자동식`, and `마흔 → 마흔 살` are also forbidden. Producer scans and Candidate v1 success never substitute for this independent check. Report actual checked scope and exact `(record_id, sense_id, candidate)` for confirmed blockers; no per-candidate receipts or second blanket semantic review are required. **Candidate follow-up rounds retain the narrow review boundary below**, rather than restarting the initial full check.
- **Risk-first review protocol:** read the *full changed-path list*, then target **(1) sentence/verb-phrase patterns, (2) lemma-containing words and phrases, (3) multiword gloss-like/collocational expressions, (4) ordinary standalone words as positive controls** against actual Canonical lemma and bound sense. Search/inspect enough changed candidates across the affected batch to establish whether a defect is systemic; do not pretend a small sample or a schema-only CI pass is a census. **Any confirmed forbidden sentence, clearly trivial form/derivation/repetition under this Stage 1 policy, or self-repetition is a `-1`; demonstrable repeated mechanical gloss restatements or disguised clauses are also production-form `-1`, consolidated by defect class, but ordinary scene/image associations are not blockers merely for being conventional**, not one comment per word. If a single questionable expression is genuinely debatable, identify it for Stage 2 instead of imposing a blanket subjective cutoff. Fix the general producer/checking rule for future batches rather than requesting only example-word substitutions.

**Candidate review-round boundary (A/B/C/D):** The **initial review** is the candidate discovery round: investigate suspicious classes sufficiently and enumerate the **exact `(record_id, sense_id, candidate)` targets** requiring correction in its first `-1`. A defect-class explanation or illustrative examples do **not** create open-ended authority to demand additional unchanged candidates in later rounds, and authors must not be told to re-audit the full batch as a review fix. **Follow-up reviews are resolution rounds**, not another batch hunt: assess only previously reported targets, the author's corrections or reasoned objections, the old-HEAD → new-HEAD candidate delta and its direct regressions. Verify every removal preserves eligible candidates; inspect newly added/modified strings. **If an unchanged, unreported candidate is noticed late, record the initial-round coverage miss as non-blocking feedback or propose separate future producer/quality work; never append that candidate to a new `-1` on the same data PR merely because it resembles an earlier class.**

**Candidate preservation and deletion-diff audit on blocker fixes (all A/B/C/D):** Removing a forbidden candidate can resolve a blocker; **removing an eligible independent word as collateral damage is a separate Stage 1 regression**. Evaluate both the corrected output and what disappeared.

- **Track the entire fix, not just the last commit:** on Candidate re-review, compare the last substantively reviewed pre-fix candidate snapshot (and the initial pre-fix snapshot when fixes span multiple commits) with the current HEAD by `(record_id, sense_id, candidate)`. Enumerate and count removed, added and replaced candidates; inspect the **negative diff** as well as surviving strings. A clean Candidate v1 validator or an author's "fully rechecked" claim does not establish that deleted candidates were ineligible.
- **Require source-bound reasons, preserve legitimate discoveries:** the author should report the removal count, actual defect classes and relevant positive controls, grouping explanations rather than writing one comment per word. A removal needs evidence that the candidate violates **Stage 1 output eligibility** (e.g. a disguised clause, routine gloss fragment, exact self-repetition or an unmistakably wrong source sense). **Not appearing as a Canonical headword/search form, being present in a gloss, unfamiliarity, different POS, weak/indirect association or low anticipated Tier is not by itself a reason to delete an independent word or compact meaning-extending scene/image/sensory expression**. Except for the **mandatory normalized source-lemma↔candidate containment exclusion**, do not purge by blanket dictionary membership, gloss-token, unrelated substring, suffix or "metadata word" filters; assess the actual lexical item and bound sense. 
- **Independently verify deletions:** group the removed set by the claimed failure mechanism, inspect the removed candidates against their exact Canonical source senses, and challenge overly broad groups with **valid standalone-word and concise scene/sensory-expression positive controls** as well as truly invalid gloss paraphrases. Expand inspection to affected removals when a broad filter is demonstrated; do not mistake sampling for exhaustive verification. If an eligible word or compact meaning-extending expression was removed without a concrete Stage 1 eligibility failure, require restoration or a substantiated correction and retain `-1` until resolved. Genuine Stage 1-ineligible strings should stay removed. **Relation Factory Stage 2, not PR Stage 1, determines the eventual relation admission/Tier of preserved words.**

This focused form gate is **separate from the lenient Stage 1 treatment of weak but intelligible word relations** and from Relation Factory Stage 2's admission/editorial judgment. Do **not** add a brittle universal regex/sub-string/word-count blocker or require all words to appear literally in a gloss.

## Interpretive boundaries and method-wide rules

- **Positive controls and interpretation:** Preserve legitimate standalone words, different POS, unusual/indirect associations, and compact scene/image/sensory/emotional expressions. Candidate-only Stage 1 checks output eligibility, **not** eventual Relation admission, Type, Tier, or artistic originality.
- **Risk-based, not a semantic census:** Detect suspicious sentences/phrases, mechanical gloss restatements, trivial derivations, and representative ordinary-word controls. Compare actual flagged outputs with the source Sense; do not inspect every ordinary word or every member of a risk class just because it can be enumerated. Expand only on concrete doubt or demonstrated failure.
- **Jamo-only rule:** For a Canonical source lemma consisting solely of independent Hangul consonant jamo (e.g. `ㄱ`, `ㄲ`, `ㄳ`, `ㅋㅋ`), the batch retains record and Sense with `candidates: []`; candidates that themselves consist solely of consonant jamo are excluded. Do not reject normal syllabic words such as `가` or `가랑잎`. This is a current shared A/B/C/D production/output rule in #860/#1155/#1156.
- **Producer validation is not independent proof:** Reuse successful Candidate v1 evidence for structural claims when sufficient, but independently check the initial whole-batch normalized source-lemma containment invariant once; do not turn this into a duplicate entire semantic pass. Do not require a producer to reconstruct private scene or corpus exploration from the PR description.

The **production** source of truth remains `docs/relation-factory.md` plus the standing method issue. A method issue does not add an extra reviewer checklist merely because it lists private exploration steps. If a demonstrated production violation needs source evidence, investigate only the specific relevant procedure; otherwise use the submitted file and bound Canonical Senses. On a follow-up, use the narrow changed/previously reported targets only, as established above.
