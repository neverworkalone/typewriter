# S2 Relation Admission Part — focused PR technical review

## Scope

Applies only to S2 Relation/Drop data-only Part PRs. Read the PR HEAD's root `REVIEW.md`; mixed implementation/data PRs use `code.md`. Editorial policy: `docs/relation-editorial-policy.md`; production contract: #927. Do not routinely reread #927.

## Relation-first review priorities

- **Admission before Type/Tier:** prioritize newly admitted Relations. Confirm each inspected path's exact source Sense, independent writer-facing expression/scene/image value, suitable Type/Tier against same-source peers, and exact target Sense or justified text-only choice. A low Tier cannot make an editorially valueless approval acceptable.
- **Same-Sense peer redundancy (all technical reviewers):** For inspected newly approved Relations, compare the target expressions **with one another**, as well as with available approved peers of the same source Sense, not only with the source lemma. Separate Relation rows that merely repeat one writer-facing choice through noun/verb ending changes, trivial derivation or spacing variation should have been `D e` rather than another approval. Keep genuinely distinct meaning, grammatical use, register or imagery; shared root/spelling alone never proves redundancy. Multiple forms within **one ID Relation row** are aliases, not separate redundant Relations. Check suspicious `e` Drops for the opposite error: loss of a useful expression. Apply this within existing source-Sense risk sampling, not a whole-Part or historical peer sweep.
- **Drops are secondary:** Stage 2 filters the broad candidate pool produced in Stage 1; the primary review risk is **over-admission**, not high Drop counts. Inspect `x`/`e` Drops selectively only when concrete source-Sense evidence suggests a useful, independent writer-facing path was wrongly excluded. Preserve plausible unusual or indirect paths. Do not select samples primarily by concentrated Drops or exhaustively inspect every Relation or Drop.
- **Risk-based sample selection:** First establish the exact current Part's source Sense scope from the Part boundaries and PR diff. Sample **newly approved Relations within that Part**, not unchanged rows from earlier Parts of the same shard; earlier approved Relations may inform peer comparisons but do not count as reviewed samples. Prioritize Senses showing potential over-admission: unusually broad or numerous approvals, incidental objects/actions, weak associations, overlapping or derivationally redundant same-Sense expressions, questionable Type/Tier or target Sense binding, and approvals defended only by a low Tier. For inspected Senses, compare approved targets with the source Sense **and one another** to decide whether some should have been `D e` or `D x` rather than Relations. Briefly state the concrete reason for selecting each risk group. Random-only samples and Drop-count-driven sampling are not sufficient risk-based S2 review. No fixed sample count, approval/Drop quota, whole-Part sweep or repeated Stage 1 candidate-form screening; expand only on concrete signs of a systematic admission defect. Judge leaked trivial candidates under S2 admission rules.
- **Follow-up scope (S2 editorial PRs):** Complete risk-based discovery in the initial Stage 1 review and identify the **specific blocked Senses/candidates** there. Subsequent Stage 1 reviews inspect only previously reported blockers, substantive author replies, and Relations/Drops actually added, changed or removed by the fix (including directly affected same-Sense peers and collateral regressions). **Do not discover or raise new editorial blockers against untouched, previously unreported Senses/candidates**, even if they show the same apparent defect class. A risk-sampled finding does not justify an open-ended demand to re-adjudicate the entire Part; state the concrete items and required corrections in the initial blocker. Once those blockers are resolved and no fix regression remains, issue `+1` without another semantic exploration pass. This does not waive the official Part validator or subsequent final HEAD/CI gate.
- **Blocking threshold:** require concrete source-Sense/peer evidence for a blocker. Minor or subjective Tier differences are non-blocking; block only evidenced, material rank inversions against same-source peers. Confirmed material admission, Sense-binding or Type errors remain blockers.

## Validation and special cases

- Reuse reliable successful official Part `verify --shard SSS --batch BB --part PP` evidence, or run the official verifier only to resolve a concrete gap. Its full structural/accounting checks still cover all Relation/Drop rows regardless of semantic review scope. Do not require unfinished sibling Parts or Batch/shard completion to pass a single Part review.
- **Proper nouns (#5745):** every candidate of a proper-noun source Sense (specific person, place, institution, organization or work per its gloss) and every candidate meaning such a proper noun must be `D e`; an ID, text-only or reverse approval of one is a blocker, and `x` is not the policy code. Judge by exact Sense/gloss, not spelling; class nouns and ordinary same-spelling Senses stay normal.
- S2 editorial Drops: `x` = unrelated/wrong Sense; `e` = connected but without independent writer value. `z` is Stage 3-only; `f` is Supervisor-only. Apply existing code-specific validation, including the `x`-only gloss-name check.
- If `f` rows exist, verify each one's source-bound single-candidate failure, one correction attempt and official save-chunk failure evidence; report their count separately from editorial `x/e` Drops.
- Authored ID links mirror source `tp`/`rv` in both directions (#2446). Equal reverse values are required, not a defect; mismatched persisted values are a blocker. Text-only links have no reverse.
