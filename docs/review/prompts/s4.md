## Initial review — every changed item

Review **every** Add, Drop, Set and Sense rebind of this PR in the actual merge-base → HEAD diff, including changes stored in other shards. `node scripts/relation/s4-cli.mjs decisions --shard SSS --base <merge-base>` lists them by relation key with the source and target meanings; `--senses <id,...>` adds every base Relation of those Senses with this PR's edit or `keep`.

- For each item check the exact source and target Sense (full gloss), independent expression value, redundancy and the representative form within the same Sense's full forward/reverse list, Type/Tier, the two-way effect of an ID Relation, and real Candidate accounting (`z` only for real orphans, `p` for removed relations, re-approval settlement).
  - **Add:** does it give a new expression choice? Exact Canonical Sense (homonyms are not chosen by spelling), text-only only when no Sense fits, no proper noun.
  - **Drop:** is no useful expression lost?
  - **Set / rebind:** is it more correct than before?
- A clear individual editing error can be a blocker. An acceptable expression choice or a minor Type/Tier preference is not.
- **Boundary of "every item":** it covers the changed relations, not a re-edit of the shard. Unchanged relations are context only, to judge a change and its direct effects. Check that edits belong to a selected `p` Sense or a valid correction of its existing Relation's binding, but do not rejudge the confirmed P/S/E choices or unchanged Senses. ## Blockers, committer replies and re-review

- **First `-1`:** give **all** blockers you found at once. For each, name the word, the exact Sense and the relation (before/after, or Add/Drop/Set) and the concrete editing error with its reasoning. Do not give a few examples and ask for a full re-judgment. No per-candidate ledger and no long Keep reports.
- The committer judges every finding independently and may change it, keep the edit with a concrete reason, or choose a better correction. A rebuttal without data changes is a valid reply.
- **Re-review** covers only the earlier blockers, the committer's actual replies, items actually changed since, and direct regressions. Do not raise new editorial blockers against words, Senses or relations that were neither reported nor changed. Do not re-review the whole earlier change set on a new HEAD. Shared integrity and direct regressions of the fix are still checked.
- **Convergence:** if the committer's position is reasonable and acceptable from the writer's point of view, withdraw the finding even if you would have decided differently or no data changed. If a rebuttal does not hold, explain the remaining problem against the actual reply and keep `-1`. Carrying out the requested change is not itself the pass condition. Once every finding is resolved and no direct regression remains, record a substantiated `+1`, also on the same HEAD.
